Every miniature begins as raw walnut, teak, or mahogany, shaped
entirely by hand across six weeks of carving, sanding, and rigging.

Our workshop follows shipbuilding techniques unchanged since
the eighteenth century — because history deserves to be told
accurately, not approximated.
