About Us

A Craft, Not a Product

Ocean Legacy Miniatures creates hand-built wooden ship miniatures for collectors, institutions and private study rooms.

Every model begins with carefully selected wood and is shaped, sanded and rigged by hand.

Our Craft

01. Solid Wood

We use materials such as walnut, teak and mahogany.

02. Hand-Tied Rigging

Rigging is made carefully to match the scale of each ship.

03. Fine Finish

Each model receives a careful final finish for display.
